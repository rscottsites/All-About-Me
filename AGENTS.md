# Project Guidelines & Rules

## Typography & Copywriting Conventions

### 1. Sentence Case Capitalization Rule
- **Headings & Subheadings (`<h1>` through `<h6>`, card headers, modal titles)**:
  - MUST use **sentence case capitalization** (only capitalize the first letter of the heading and proper nouns/acronyms such as WCAG, ARIA, PDF, Ryan Scott, RScott Sites, iOS, Android, HTML5, NVDA, VoiceOver, TalkBack).
  - *Do NOT use Title Case* (e.g., use `"Why accessibility matters to your bottom line"` instead of `"Why Accessibility Matters to Your Bottom Line"`).
- **Buttons & Call-to-Action (CTA) Links**:
  - MUST use **sentence case capitalization** (e.g., `"Request a free mini-audit"`, `"Download free PDF guide"`, `"View services & pricing"`, `"Read guide online"`).
  - *Do NOT use Title Case* (e.g., avoid `"Request A Free Mini-Audit"`).
- **Applies to**: Current codebase and all future components, pages, data files, modals, and additions.
